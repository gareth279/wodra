# Wodra XC Skills Loop (fictional)

## Scope and provenance

This is a fictional 1.6 km closed cross-country training course authored for Wodra. It is not based on a surveyed or public trail and makes no claim of geographic fidelity. The route centerline, analytic heightfield, vegetation positions, berms, rollers, and line-choice shapes are deterministic original procedural content generated by `tools/Wodra.RouteBuilder/generate-xc-skills-loop.py`. No external geographic or asset dataset is used. The rider and bike use Wodra's existing model.

The reference route is 1,600 m and has 26.0 m of positive ascent in its authored elevation profile. The course includes sustained gentle climbing and descending rather than an artificial climb followed by a descent. The 2 m-spaced centerline is closed at the lap seam. The procedural terrain is for visual context, not an external DEM or survey. The singletrack ribbon is an authored presentation width, not a measured trail width.

## Line choices and progress

Three optional tabletop side lines begin at reference chainages 210 m, 730 m, and 1,250 m. Each occupies a 150 m route-relative window and reconnects at its authored end. The plan-view side-line path is 150.22 m; its smooth 0.22 m detour is represented through route-relative lateral offsets. A sign 36 m before each split indicates the optional jump line; straight riding is the natural bypass. Berms are placed at 430–478 m, 970–1,024 m, and 1,082–1,128 m. Two small rollers are placed at 520–540 m and 1,150–1,172 m.

Ride progress and lap boundaries use the 1,600 m canonical reference line. The standard active ride clock remains authoritative and freezes during pause; this MVP does not create a separate timed effort or lap PB. The lap summary reports only line count and optional challenge score. The rider steers inside a route-relative corridor that follows the authored mainline and widens only as an optional branch opens, capped at 3.25 m from the reference line. Each side line is a route-relative lateral path over its stated chainage window; its roughly 0.22 m extra plan-view distance is not separately added to cycling distance. Splits begin and end at the same centerline chainages, so crossing and rejoining do not teleport or skip progress. At a lap boundary, route distance continues monotonically and feature scoring keys include lap number. Repositioning, reverse travel, or a discontinuity cannot score. Because existing progress does not encode branch identity or branch-specific timing, this route explicitly has no ghost/PB comparisons, pacers, AI riders, or multiplayer.

At low virtual speed, the rider follows the same authored raised trail surface without virtual airtime; at or above the feature's 20 km/h threshold, the visual observer adds a small take-off/landing arc based on progress through the authored line. This is visual-only. The underlying 26.0 m ascent, resistance, distance, and FIT samples are unchanged. Gaming optionally enables feature points and feedback; the course remains rideable with Gaming disabled. Feature points are challenge score only, not cycling metrics or timed effort results.

## Regeneration and packaging

Run `python tools/Wodra.RouteBuilder/generate-xc-skills-loop.py` from the repository root. The four generated sidecars are embedded in the desktop assembly and included in the portable route package. Rebuild the route package and run the publisher route-package validation after regeneration.
