# Wodra v0.1.0-alpha.54

Fix local rider/ambient car pass-through in the production ride scene. The local rider's rendered envelope is swept against nearby car motion before route progress is applied; if clearance would be violated, only local ride progress and virtual speed are constrained. Steering is also checked against occupied car envelopes so a rider cannot steer into a car while passing.

Validation includes a production RideScreen reproduction with a controlled slower car on Signal Hill, a clear passing maneuver, and regressions for narrow-road following, steering into an occupied envelope, vertical road separation, curved routes, and large frame steps. The rider remained out of the car envelope throughout the interactive passing run. Existing ambient car following/overtaking, AI/remote rider handling, camera, trainer telemetry, and activity recording behavior are preserved.

Issue #28 remains open pending owner physical acceptance testing. Automated scene validation does not replace testing on the owner's Windows setup.
