# Wodra v0.1.0-alpha.58

## Pacer HUD polish

- Hide the in-ride pacer panel for rides started with No pacer, while keeping its visibility consistent for the full session.
- Keep the compact selector and pacer controls available throughout pacer-enabled rides, including after Stop following.
- Place the pacer panel at the bottom right and size the ride action bar to its controls.
- Preserve the accepted offline pacer simulation, rendezvous, trainer control, ride measurements, and recording behavior.

The production Oval–Gardens ride scene was reviewed at default, minimum, and fullscreen sizes with debug and dummy-trainer controls shown and hidden. Validation used the dummy trainer; physical trainer hardware was not tested.
