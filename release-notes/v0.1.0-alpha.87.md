# Wodra v0.1.0-alpha.87

## Rider customisation

- Choose a jersey colour per rider profile: Teal, Navy blue, Red, Green, White, or Orange. Dark cycling shorts remain unchanged.
- Choose a helmet colour per rider profile: Charcoal, White, Blue, Red, Teal, or the original purple/blue finish. Straps, clasp, and retention details stay dark.
- New and older profiles default to a Teal jersey and Charcoal helmet. Choices persist with profile data and are used in rides.
- Adjusted the rigid helmet shell by 3° forward pitch for a closer forehead fit while preserving the accepted orientation, supplied geometry, and single-piece assembly.

## Compatibility

- Existing rider profiles, activities, ride history, FIT recordings, settings, device preferences, and authentication data are retained.
