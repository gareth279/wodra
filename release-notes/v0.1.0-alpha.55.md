# Wodra v0.1.0-alpha.55

## Ride physics invariant and ambient traffic

- Removed PR #105's ambient-car constraints on local ride progress, virtual speed and steering. Ambient traffic cannot change the authoritative rider simulation or recorded distance.
- Ambient cars now yield toward the road centre when a faster local rider is approaching and the short swept corridor is clear. Cars return smoothly to their travel lane after the rider passes.
- Car spawn selection avoids placing a slow car directly ahead when the local rider would catch it immediately. Cars refuse to yield into an occupied corridor.
- The alpha.53 vehicle following, overtaking, junction and lane-return changes from PR #104 remain in place. AI and remote rider motion, rider-to-rider separation, camera, trainer controls and activity recording are unchanged.

Automated validation compares identical deterministic watts, gradient, elapsed-time, steering, rider/bike and trainer traces with a slow car active and with traffic absent, including narrow roads, bends, high-power downhill riding and large frame steps. A production RideScreen test covers the local rider catching a slower car. Cars may be unable to clear a narrow or occupied road; in that case the player simulation remains unrestricted and scenery overlap may still occur.

Issue #28 remains open pending owner physical acceptance testing.
