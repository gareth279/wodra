# Wodra v0.1.0-alpha.88

## Fixes

- Keep the in-ride Settings and Menu modal above media controls. The modal backdrop now covers the media panel and handles input while the modal is open; closing the menu restores the media controls to their existing position.
- Add production RideScreen regression coverage that inspects skinned shoe geometry and forefoot contact through a complete crank cycle. In Godot 4.6.2, both shoe meshes point toe-forward and remain on the pedals, so the accepted shoe geometry and improved leg rig are preserved without a speculative bone rotation.
- Verify the alpha.87 helmet pitch adjustment in production side, front, and three-quarter views. The 3° forward pitch improves the fit; helmet yaw and front/rear orientation remain unchanged.

## Validation

- Windows Debug and Release builds.
- Production RideScreen visual regression captures at eight 45° crank phases, helmet pitch comparison at 0°/3°/6°, and alternate jersey/helmet colours.
- Media modal layering regression at 960×540, 1280×720, 1920×1080, and 2560×1440.
