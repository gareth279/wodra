## Offline pacer riders

- Select no pacer, Easy (1.5 W/kg), Moderate (2.2 W/kg), or Strong (3.0 W/kg) before a ride.
- Target watts use a documented 75 kg assumed rider and 8 kg bike. Pacers follow Wodra's cycling physics and route gradients, with continuous route distance and lap progression.
- Pacer identity, effort and relative gap appear in the ride view, nearby-rider display and route inset. Pacers work offline and use existing AI cyclist visuals.
- Player physics, trainer commands, measured telemetry and FIT/Strava activity inputs remain independent of pacer state.
