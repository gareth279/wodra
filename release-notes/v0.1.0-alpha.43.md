# Wodra 0.1.0-alpha.43

This release improves camera clearance around scenery and adds continuous turns at exact reverse retraces in the South Coastal route.

- The camera now checks a volume that covers its near-plane clearance, including its full movement from the previous frame. It retracts immediately when scenery blocks the view and recovers smoothly after the path clears. Camera diagnostics include the selected view, desired and resolved camera positions, collision source and clearance result.
- South Coastal now has width-bounded turnarounds at the two mapped reverse retraces. The route retains its identity and gains route data version 2 so saved history and comparisons remain associated with the same route identity while incompatible geometry fingerprints remain distinguishable.
- Route and scenery packages were regenerated from the 18 October 2026 Geofabrik New Zealand extract. OpenStreetMap attribution and the source checksum are recorded in the authoring documentation and generated report.
