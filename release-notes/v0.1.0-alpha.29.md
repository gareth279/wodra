# Wodra v0.1.0-alpha.29

- The connection screen now presents trainer, optional heart-rate, and steering/controller cards. Trainer readiness remains independent of optional peripherals.
- Initial ANT+ pairing gives the remembered FE-C trainer 26 seconds, then falls back to fresh discovery. A verified candidate starts a bounded three-second settling period; a full search remains when no eligible trainer appears. In-ride recovery timing and selected-trainer pinning are unchanged.
- ANT+ network-key entry and maintenance moved to Settings → Devices → Advanced ANT+ setup. Existing local configuration is retained.
- Added Bluetooth LE Heart Rate Service discovery and connection, single-device auto-selection, multiple-device selection, fresh BPM display, and heart-rate input to the ride HUD and FIT recording.
- Elite steering/controller connection is not enabled until the exact device model and Bluetooth protocol are identified.

## Verification

- EOS-enabled Windows Debug and Release builds passed; automated lifecycle, discovery-policy, heart-rate parsing/selection, HUD/FIT freshness, and existing regression checks passed.
- Hardware verification of faster pairing, Bluetooth heart-rate sensors, and steering is pending. The supplied alpha.28 rides do not verify recovery or physical resistance behavior for this build.
