# Wodra v0.1.0-alpha.24

- ANT+ FE-C discovery now sends a nonblocking `CHANNEL_ID (0x51)` request after ordinary trainer telemetry arrives, buffers bounded page evidence while the wildcard identity is pending, and retries that request at most three times.
- The ANT PC SDK 3.5 routes the channel-ID response through `deviceResponse`; Wodra now consumes it there and correlates it to the active channel and scan attempt. It continues to accept ordinary `0x4E` broadcasts and extended source-ID frames.
- The connection panel distinguishes received FE-C data with unresolved identity from a scan that received no trainer pages.
- Bluetooth FTMS, the ANT+ setup screen, saved key configuration, and readiness requirements are preserved.

The alpha.23 cycling-PC log showed nine page `0x10` and nine page `0x19` broadcasts rejected as `source_id_missing`. SDK source inspection confirmed that alpha.23 neither requested channel identity nor listened for its response on the SDK callback that receives it. Regression coverage exercises the request/response coordination with ordinary SDK-format pages, both callback orderings, bounded retry, wrong-channel and zero identities, and stale attempts.

The new ANT request/response path has not yet been accepted on the Flux 2. Headless builds, simulated frames, or command acknowledgements do not establish hardware telemetry, resistance control, or recovery. Issues #3 and #4 remain open pending the bike-PC retest.
