# Wodra 0.1.0-alpha.42

This focused ride-polish prerelease adds shared camera obstruction handling and corrects stale roadside elevation data across the three additional Dunedin route packages.

## Ride and scenery fixes

- Chase cameras now query the generated building and terrain collision geometry. They retract to a clear point before an obstruction and ease back to the selected view after it clears.
- Building footprints are omitted only where their horizontal footprint and vertical volume intersect the 7 m riding corridor. Elevated crossings and buildings on other levels remain in the scene.
- Re-sampled the Rolling City, Signal Hill, and South Coastal street bundles and roadside objects from the cached LINZ DEM using sampler version 2. This corrects stale street heights without changing mapped street geometry or route chainage.
- Documented the shared route identity, geometry fingerprint, metre chainage, coordinate frame, width defaults, scenery provenance, and optional future observation references in `docs/route-geometry-contract.md`.

## Validation and follow-up

- Debug and Release solution builds passed.
- Route-builder, physics, multiplayer protocol, and visual-route regression suites passed.
- Godot headless world construction passed for all four bundled routes, including building and terrain collision meshes.
- Graphical ride inspection was unavailable in this environment. Owner review remains open for Rolling City at 450–600 m, the South Coastal turnarounds, Signal Hill near 10,924.5 m, and representative sections on every route.
- South Coastal route points retrace the same mapped road in reverse at two endpoint turns; this package does not alter canonical path length or pretend camera smoothing fixes rider movement. Issue #19 remains open for a road-space turnaround geometry update and regenerated dependent route assets.
- Signal Hill's candidate crossing surfaces near 10,924.5 m map to OSM roads Ramsay Street (way 115698350) and Gladstone Road (way 511253876). Their DEM elevations are above the route at that point, so they were preserved as possible legitimate crossings pending visual inspection.
