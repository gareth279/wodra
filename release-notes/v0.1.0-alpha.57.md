# Wodra v0.1.0-alpha.57

## Offline pacer improvements

- Identify Easy, Moderate, and Strong offline pacers with colored Wodra cat markers that match the ride selector and route map.
- Choose a pacer during a ride and see its target effort and live gap, including when no pacer is currently selected.
- Bring the selected local pacer to a safe nearby position on the same route, then ride together at its normal target effort.
- Keep player movement, trainer control, measured telemetry, and recorded ride data independent of pacer position.

Offline pacers do not require an EOS connection. This release was exercised with the dummy trainer; physical trainer hardware has not been validated.
