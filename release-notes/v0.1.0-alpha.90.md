# Wodra v0.1.0-alpha.90

## Changes

- Moved the selected cycling helmet 10 mm forward while keeping its 12° forward pitch, existing vertical fit, supplied shell and materials.
- Improved helmet strap routing around the ears.
- Added separately fitted jersey and bib-short meshes with jersey color customization, cleaner hems and shorts grippers.
- Corrected jersey color contamination on the Human face and jaw.
- Retained the alpha.89 shoe and ankle correction and the existing rider pedal animation.
- Reworked Windows update handling around one application-lifetime service shared by startup and Settings. Checks and downloads remain visible when screens change, feed failures can be retried, interrupted downloads retain their offer, and staged updates wait until rides and FIT replay are finished.

## Known issue

The jersey neckline remains jagged and the fitted stand-up cycling collar is unfinished. This cosmetic limitation is accepted for alpha.90 and is tracked for later manual Blender work in [issue #173](https://github.com/gareth279/wodra/issues/173). The alpha.90 release does not claim to deliver a finished collar.

## Installation

The Windows installer uses a per-user installation and does not require administrator privileges. Portable Windows and Velopack packages are also provided. The Alpha update channel remains unchanged.
