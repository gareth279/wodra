# Wodra v0.1.0-alpha.52

## Changes

- Ambient vehicles now account for cyclist, pedestrian and vehicle model envelopes along curved route paths. Cars brake smoothly behind occupied traffic and only overtake when the road and the predicted passing corridor are clear; otherwise they follow.
- AI cyclists now follow slower riders and select smoothly reached lateral slots when riders would otherwise intersect. This includes the local rider, other AI riders and interpolated remote riders. Replay ghosts remain non-colliding.
- Low-frame-rate vehicle movement is clamped before the swept actor envelopes would intersect. Ambient actors also reject overlapping spawn positions.
- Added production RideScreen and deterministic traffic regressions for road-width-dependent passing, groups, stopped and slower riders, curves, crowded pass lanes, pedestrian/vehicle conflicts, grade separation, large frame deltas, camera views and actor model bounds.

## Verification

- Windows Debug and Release solution builds and the CI regression suites passed locally, except the PHP broker check, which is provisioned by Windows CI and unavailable in this environment.
- Godot 4.6.2 production traffic and RideScreen review scenes passed on Rolling City with 4 AI riders, 15 cars and 10 pedestrians. The actual imported rider model bounds were checked across camera transitions and 90 sampled frames; minimum measured cyclist-to-cyclist and actor-to-cyclist clearances were 17.194 m and 1.941 m respectively.
- Physical acceptance remains pending. Issues #28 and #102 remain open. Remote multiplayer positions are still authoritative on their owning peers; this release smooths local remote-rider presentation only and does not add server-authoritative collision resolution.
- Alpha.50 device discovery continues to require owner testing with real Bluetooth/ANT+ hardware. No hardware acceptance is claimed here.

## Known acceptance limits

- Automated path and production-scene checks do not replace owner riding tests on real routes and hardware.
- Multiplayer collision authority is not centralized; owner testing should watch for cross-client visual disagreement when both peers steer into the same space.
