# Wodra 0.1.0-alpha.45

This alpha adds the accepted stylised low-poly rider and refined road bicycle.

- Replaces the placeholder cyclist with a complete weighted human mesh and
  skeleton, fitted cycling kit, optional helmet, and cadence-driven pedal
  animation. Pedalling supports coasting and restart while preserving hand,
  saddle, and pedal contacts.
- Refines the unbranded road-bike cockpit, compact brake hoods, frame junctions,
  pedals, and moderately deep dark carbon rims. Wheel rotation remains tied to
  road speed independently of rider cadence; bike fit anchors are kept separate
  from shared rider animation for future bike types.
- Includes editable Blender source, repeatable MPFB-to-GLB instructions, and
  Godot review stills and motion clips. The owner approved the rider and road
  bike for this alpha on 20 October 2026.

The implementation was validated by Windows Debug/Release CI, CodeQL, the
Blender baked-cycle and GLB inspections, Godot route-lighting captures, and the
project regression suites. MTB/gravel/track models and the remaining OpenSim
reference-model review are future work.
