# Wodra v0.1.0-alpha.89

## Fixes

- Correct the anatomical shoe/ankle attachment. The lower-leg/foot joint now aligns with the measured heel-side ankle opening while preserving toe-forward orientation, forefoot contact at the pedal spindle, and the improved knee motion.
- Raise the jersey clothing mask through the neck base to cover the upper chest, add a fitted 15 mm collar using the selected jersey color, and place the zipper seam on the jersey front.
- Retain the improved 12° forward helmet pitch and preserve its original physical orientation, rigid assembly, straps, and configurable colors.

- Windows Debug and Release solution builds.
- Godot 4.6.2 production RideScreen fit regression across 72 crank samples, with physical shoe feature, ankle-opening, forefoot contact, upper-chest coverage, collar, and jersey color checks.
- Godot 4.6.2 production motion regression across 480 frames covering cadence, steering, camera changes, coasting, stopping, and restart.
- Production side, front, rear, and three-quarter visual evidence: [alpha.89 rider visual evidence](../docs/alpha89-rider-visual-evidence.md).
