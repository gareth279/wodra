# Wodra v0.1.0-alpha.94

## Changes
- Shared footprint-based road corridors keep hedges, fences, tree trunks, buildings and furniture clear of rideable roads and the cyclist envelope while retaining permissible canopy overhang and legitimate road markings.
- Pine Hill roadside scenery uses measured occupied footprints; the long hedge and other intrusions are repositioned from full-geometry clearance checks. Nine unmarked crossings no longer generate fictional zebra paint.
- Generic mapped-road paint is opt-in; explicitly tagged zebra crossings, controls and authored markings remain supported.
- MTB XC Skills Loop tabletop approaches, take-off lips and landings are sculpted into the trail terrain. Bike pitch and airborne movement are visible, with scored jump events and continuous rollable bypasses.
- Jump event detection and scoring are validated in RideScreen. Steering-based collectible pickups remain accessible; lap pickup counts are separated from cumulative totals.
- Shared terrain normals remove coarse terrain facets across MTB and Gravel without changing source elevation data or route geometry.
- Offline route-quality audits, geometric scenery regressions and production RideScreen coverage have been expanded.

## Validation
- PR #178 Debug, Release, CodeQL and source-analysis checks passed.
- Production RideScreen XC, Gravel and road/MTB/velodrome transition regressions passed on Godot 4.6.2 Compatibility renderer.
- The package includes Pine Hill Test, Gravel routes and MTB XC Skills Loop route data and sidecars.

## Known limitations
- Physical trainer and Sterzo acceptance remains outstanding. Historical before/after performance baseline is unavailable; no performance delta is claimed.
